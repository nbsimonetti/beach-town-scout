# Baja California Sur Deep Screen — "Next Tulum / Next 30A" for a DFW Buyer
*Research date: Aug 2026. All $ = USD. Estimates computed by the researcher are flagged [calc] or [est].*

## 1. Todos Santos & Pescadero (incl. Cerritos)

**Flight access from DFW:** Via SJD (Los Cabos): AA is the only nonstop DFW–SJD carrier, 10x/week, ~3h11m. SJD is Mexico's 6th-busiest airport, 7.53M pax 2025. Drive SJD → Todos Santos ~1h15–1h30 via 4-lane Hwy 19. Door-to-door DFW ~7–8h [est] (SJD's notorious 90-min immigration lines).

**Prices & 5-yr trend:** Market span $18K entry lots → $30M beachfront estates; median ~$370K. AMPI-style sales data (Protect Todos Santos 2025): homes sold peaked 140 (2021) → 71 (2024); avg home price peak $1.046M (2022) → $825K (2024), −21%. Condos (mostly Cerritos): avg $887.5K (2023) → $334K (2024), −62% as new Cerritos supply hit. BHHS Q1-2025: median home $500K (+8.2%); median land $125K (+78.6%). Big 2020–2022 COVID run-up, then correction; corridor led BCS with 30% of all lots sold 2019–2024.

**Rental economics:** AirDNA (Jul 2026): 614 listings, ADR $241, occupancy 42%, avg annual revenue $16.4K; YoY revenue −21.1%. Gross yield [calc]: ~3.3% on $500K median home; ~4.9% on $334K condo. Brokerage "8–10% ROI" claims are promotional.

**STR regulation:** BCS 3% ISH lodging tax; state registration; 2025–26 enforcement crackdown on platform tax evasion. No permit caps yet.

**Foreign ownership/title:** Fideicomiso (~$500–1K setup, ~$500–700/yr); closing 6–10% incl. 2–4.5% acquisition tax. Ejido risk real: Pescadero/Cerritos was largely ejido farmland; improperly regularized parcels carry lingering claims. MLS stock generally titled; off-market "cheap" lots are the trap. Stewart/First American title insurance ~0.5–0.7%.

**Financing:** Mostly cash. MoXi 30-yr fixed USD $250K–$2.5M, max 65% LTV, ~high-8s to low-10s%. Leverage kills ~3% gross yields.

**Safety:** BCS Level 2, but deteriorating: Apr 2025 US Embassy alert (bus burnings, Cabo shootout, law-enforcement murders); BCS homicides +124% Jan–Aug 2025. Violence targeted, not tourist-directed; Todos Santos itself low-crime.

**Infrastructure:** Water is the #1 constraint — seawater intrusion documented 1.6 km inland, chloride above potable limits near coast; many new builds on trucked water. $370M SJD expansion 2025–2029.

**Momentum:** Established Pueblo Mágico brand; Todos Santos Boutique Hotel at $1,343/night (2024); Cerritos fastest-growing on BCS Pacific coast. But maturing — discovery phase was 2015–2022.

**Supply:** Aquifer is the binding constraint, not zoning; large ejido tracts coming to market — land supply NOT tight (why condos cracked).

**Hazards:** Direct hurricane corridor (Norma 2023, Lorena/Raymond 2025). No sargassum (Pacific). Moderate seismicity.

**Bull:** The proven "30A of Baja" one hour from a 7.5M-pax airport with 10 weekly DFW nonstops — buy the dip (condos −62% off peak). **Bear:** You're late; aquifer salting, condo oversupply, ejido landmines, 3% gross yields — lifestyle asset, not investment.

**Scores:** DFW access 8 · Value 6 · Momentum 5 · Catalysts 7 · Risk drag 4 → **30/50**

## 2. La Ventana / El Sargento

**Flight access:** No DFW nonstop to LAP (one-stops only; LAP 1.34M pax 2025) then ~40–45 min drive; or DFW–SJD nonstop + ~2h15–2h45 drive. Door-to-door ~8–9.5h either way. Weakest DFW access of the three.

**Prices & 5-yr trend:** ~80 active listings: lots $20K–$4.6M (typical $41K Los Planes); condos $198K–$420K+; houses $219K entry / ~$698K mid / $2.8M top. Broker typicals: land $120K–170K (stable 3 yrs); finished homes $425K–600K; beachfront $900K–$2M. BHHS Q1-2025: median home $650K (−4.7%); land volume +656.6% YoY, median land price +438% — a land rush (small sample). Lots roughly doubled since ~2019–2020 [est, low confidence]. Active foreign-buyer band $150K–600K.

**Rental economics:** AirDNA (Jul 2026): 234 listings, ADR $155, occupancy 48%, avg annual revenue $11.6K. Extreme seasonality: Nov–Apr kite season is the whole market. Gross yield [calc]: ~2.7% on $425K home; ~5.3% on $220K casita. Monthly winter rentals to kiters/remote workers are the real model.

**STR regulation:** Same BCS regime; village-level enforcement essentially nil today.

**Title — THE key risk:** Much of El Sargento/La Ventana was ejido; titling moved from beachfront inland. Untitled land sells via constancia ejidal at fractions of titled prices; clients have waited 20 years for title. Example: $95K untitled vs $190K appraised titled. Buy titled + title policy only.

**Financing:** Overwhelmingly cash; many properties below MoXi's $250K loan minimum. Some seller financing on lots.

**Safety:** Level 2; Apr 2025 alert covered La Paz 40 min away; village itself very low crime.

**Infrastructure:** Dirt/partially paved roads improving; many off-grid solar; no hospital (La Paz 40 min). Starlink ubiquitous. Since 2013: restaurants, farmers market, pharmacies, Montessori school.

**Momentum:** World top-3 winter kite/wing destination; growing year-round digital-nomad residency; land sales surging. No branded hotels, no celebrity press wave yet = earlier on the curve.

**Supply:** Beachfront strip titled and largely built/held; expansion runs uphill into ejido land that can't legally transfer until regularized — a genuine supply lock supporting titled-land values.

**Hazards:** Sea of Cortez hurricane exposure; arroyo flash floods; no sargassum; moderate seismic.

**Bull:** Best pure "next Tulum at year-2012 prices" setup in BCS — famous wind-sports anchor, 40 min from a growing capital airport, titled supply locked by ejido, lots still $120–170K with land volume +656%. **Bear:** One-season market (48% occ, $155 ADR, dead summers), no DFW nonstop, 9-hour door-to-door, ejido minefields one street back, niche-sport demand may not scale.

**Scores:** DFW access 4 · Value 7 · Momentum 7 · Catalysts 7 · Risk drag 5 → **30/50**

## 3. Loreto

**Flight access:** AA (Envoy E175) flies DFW–LTO nonstop, seasonal: ~3h; ~1x/week rising to ~4x/week by July 2026. Airport ~10 min from town → door-to-door ~5.5–6.5h when nonstop runs (fastest of the three); off-season one-stop via PHX ~8h. LTO traffic 116.8K (2019) → 183.4K (2025), +57%.

**Prices & 5-yr trend:** Median up ~60% since 2021: ~$325K → $500K+; 2025 transaction volume a 4-year high (much volume off-MLS/developer-direct). Range $150K–$7M+; Costa Loreto new product $600K–$3M+.

**Rental economics:** AirROI: 350 listings, occupancy 31.5%, ADR $151, avg annual revenue $12.5K, RevPAR $50; revenue −10.4% YoY; supply +29.6% YoY. Gross yield [calc]: ~2.5% on $500K median. Weakest STR economics of the three — supply growing faster than demand.

**Title:** Cleanest of the three — core supply is FONATUR-master-planned Nopoló/Loreto Bay land and long-permitted developer parcels (SEMARNAT authorizations, marina concession 2006); less raw-ejido exposure in buyable stock.

**Financing:** Cash and developer financing dominate.

**Safety:** Quietest sizable town in BCS, far from the La Paz/Cabo cartel corridor; lowest practical security risk of the three.

**Infrastructure:** Airport 10 min; rebuilt Marina Puerto Escondido (80+ slips); Danzante Bay new roads/utilities. 6h drive from Cabo — isolation real.

**Momentum:** Strongest current story: prices +60% since 2021, 4-yr-high transactions, plus supply wave — Danzante Bay/Mantarraya + TPC Danzante Bay golf (4,447-acre Villa Group resort), Costa Loreto marina-village, Nopolo Hills. Buyer profile: "people who've done Cabo looking for what's next."

**Supply:** Genuinely constrained medium-term (Sierra de la Giganta behind, Bahía de Loreto National Marine Park in front) but near-term land inventory has multiplied since 2021.

**Hazards:** Sea of Cortez hurricane track; arroyo flooding; moderate seismic; no sargassum. Single-runway seasonal airport = concentration risk for the whole thesis.

**Bull:** Only town of the three with a true "next-resort" institutional build-out (TPC golf, 80-slip marina, FONATUR bones, +57% airport traffic) plus DFW nonstop going 1x→4x weekly — classic 10-years-behind-Cabo setup with clean developer title. **Bear:** Worst STR fundamentals (31.5% occ, RevPAR $50, supply +30% YoY), the +60% pop took the easy gains, and if AA cuts the seasonal E175 you own a 6-hour-drive-from-anywhere market.

**Scores:** DFW access 6 · Value 6 · Momentum 8 · Catalysts 8 · Risk drag 6 → **34/50**
