# Caribbean "Next Tulum / Next 30A" Screen — Puerto Rico & Dominican Republic (Aug 2026)

*Items marked ~est.~ are best-effort estimates, flagged.*

## 1. Puerto Rico West Coast — Rincón / Aguadilla / Isabela

**Access:** DFW–SJU nonstop: AA + Frontier, ~16 weekly (2+ daily), ~4h30m–5h. Then SJU→Rincón drive ~2.5h. Door-to-door ~9–10h. BQN (Aguadilla, 20–30 min from Rincón/Isabela): no DFW nonstop; JetBlue FLL/JFK, new Avelo HVN/LAL from Nov 2026; one-stops improving fast — itself a catalyst signal. **BQN terminal expansion bid opened July 17, 2026** (PR Ports Authority RFP).

**Prices:** Rincón median ~$385K; broader listing median $575–600K (2025); oceanfront $700K–$1.5M; surf-adjacent luxury $800K–$3M. PR luxury +11.6% YoY Q1 2025; +6–14% YoY Q1 2026 by location. West coast still ~60% cheaper than Dorado/Condado luxury. Rincón values roughly doubled 2019→2025 ~est.~ (Zillow 403-blocked). Aguadilla = airport town, Ramey Base area (flat, titled, ex-military housing popular with Act 60 crowd), better value; Isabela = Jobos/Shacks surf + newer villas, mid-priced, most raw land.

**Rentals (AirDNA 2026):** Rincón 1,628 active listings, ADR $247, occupancy 50%, avg revenue $28.6K, RevPAR ~$126. Market Score 81/100. YoY: revenue −2.8%, listings −3.8% — saturation real but plateauing. On $385K median: ~7.4% gross yield (better on Aguadilla/Isabela basis).

**STR regulation:** PRTC innkeeper registration + 7% room tax on <90-day stays; fines to $500/day / $25K max. **Act 60 verified:** Law 66-2025 excluded STRs (<90 days) from the rental-income exemption; new Act 60 applicants from 2026 pay 4% (not 0%) on passive income, existing decrees grandfathered; program extended to 2055. Senate Bill 238 (2025): centralized municipal STR registry pending. No Rincón-specific ordinance — west coast currently light-touch.

**Ownership:** US fee simple, domestic transaction, US courts, US title insurance. Quirks: informal/unpermitted construction, CRIM record mismatches, unsegregated family land — full title study + surveyor standard.

**Financing/insurance:** Conventional + FHA/VA available; Banco Popular, FirstBank, Oriental; closings 60–90 days ~est.~. Insurance: post-Maria elevated; ~$3–6K/yr on a $400K coastal home ~est.~; hurricane deductibles 2–5%.

**Safety:** West coast far safer feel than San Juan metro; Rincón/Isabela = petty theft, car break-ins at surf lots.

**Infrastructure — the #1 drag: the grid.** Island-wide blackouts NYE Dec 31 2024 (1.4M+ customers) and April 16, 2025 (Easter week, 1.4M); LUMA projected ~154 hours of outages Jun 2024–Jun 2025; only ~53% of generation reliably available; reconstruction stalled. **Solar + battery effectively mandatory capex (~$25–40K ~est.~) and now a listing feature.** Cisterns standard.

**Supply:** Coastal Zone Management + maritime-terrestrial zone limits true beachfront builds; permitting (OGPe) slow/litigious (Sol y Playa condo controversy); buildable titled coastal land genuinely scarce in Rincón; more available Isabela/Aguadilla.

**Hazards:** Direct-hit hurricane island (Maria 2017, Fiona 2022). Seismic: 2020 M6.4 swarm hit the SW; west coast near Mona Passage faults. Sargassum: Rincón largely spared (west/NW-facing); record 2025 Atlantic year hit mostly east/south shores.

**Bull:** US-domestic legal/financing rails + Act 60 to 2055 + BQN airlift expansion on a coast still 60% below Dorado — the closest thing to "30A twenty years ago" you can buy with a Fannie Mae loan. **Bear:** A grid that fully blacked out twice in four months, hurricane/quake corridor, STR revenue already flat at 1,600+ listings — the easy STR money is behind; carry costs (insurance + solar) eat yield.
**Scores:** Access 7 | Value 6 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 7 | Risk 4 → **31**

## 2. Dominican Republic — Las Terrenas (Samaná)

**Access:** No good direct gateway. Best: DFW→(MIA/CLT)→SDQ + 2h30m drive via the Samaná toll road. Door-to-door ~11–13h. **El Catey (AZS), 30 min away, has NO US service** (only Air Canada/Transat/WestJet + Air Caraïbes Paris). A future US route into AZS is the single biggest option-value catalyst — it doesn't exist yet.

**Prices:** $1,500–2,500/sqm condos; $2,000–3,500/sqm beachfront; land $1,000–1,500/sqm; entry ~$180K, median transaction ~$290K. Appreciation 8–10%/yr past 5 yrs, above DR national average; recent +10.7% YoY apartments, +11.6% houses; beach-proximate +15–20% recent years (AreaVista 2026).

**Rentals:** Occupancy 32–42% (AirROI 32.4%, AreaVista 42%), ADR $131–236 (median $130–155; top 10% >$450), avg revenue $19–24K, 2,192 listings; Dec–Apr peak 70–90% for premium units; net yields "can exceed 7%" managed but AirROI ranks Las Terrenas bottom 15% of Caribbean STR yield. Highly seasonal, Euro-skewed — buy for appreciation + peak-season cashflow, not year-round yield.

**STR/tax:** STRs essentially unregulated nationally. **CONFOTUR (Law 158-01):** approved-project buyers get 3% transfer-tax exemption + 15-yr exemption from 1% annual property tax (IPI) + rental income-tax relief in window. 2024–26 fiscal-reform chatter has floated trimming exemptions — watch.

**Title:** Foreigners hold fee simple, same rights as Dominicans; Torrens-style registered title. Buy only deslinde-completed title; avoid constancia anotada (undivided interest); Dominican attorney mandatory.

**Financing:** Majority-cash; developer pre-construction financing common (20–50% down, 2–5 yrs); local banks lend to foreigners with residency/income docs ~8–12%, 50–70% LTV ~est.~. Assume cash.

**Safety:** Samaná among the lowest-crime DR provinces (a recent quarterly police dataset showed zero armed robberies/vehicle thefts); large French/European expat base; DR overall Level 2.

**Infrastructure/momentum:** Samaná toll road done. **Marriott Autograph "Donoma Las Terrenas" (94-room oceanfront) opened Nov 2025 — first international hotel brand in town** — classic institutional-arrival signal. DR tourism records: 3.73M US air visitors Jan–Oct 2025 (52% of arrivals).

**Supply:** Town pinched between the Cordillera Samaná and the beach; limited flat land behind Playa Bonita/Las Ballenas/Cosón; low-rise norms.

**Hazards:** North coast historically fewer direct hurricane hits but exposed; **Septentrional fault runs along the north coast** — major long-interval seismic risk rarely priced in; sargassum intermittent/lighter than the east ~est.~ (2025–26 worst basin year on record; DR sought UNESCO emergency declaration Jun 2025).

**Bull:** Marriott's arrival + 8–12%/yr prints + CONFOTUR tax-free carry in the DR's prettiest, safest beach town, with a US route into 30-min-away El Catey as unpriced option value — the cleanest "Tulum 2012" analog on the board. **Bear:** 11–13h door-to-door with no US airlift, 32–42% occupancy in a bottom-quartile-yield market, cash-only underwriting, uninsured seismic/hurricane tail.
**Scores:** Access 4 | Value 6 | Momentum 8 | Catalysts 8 | Risk 5 → **31**

## 3. Dominican Republic — Cabarete / Encuentro / Sosúa / Cabrera (north coast)

**Access:** POP (Puerto Plata) 20–30 min from Cabarete/Sosúa; no DFW nonstop — via MIA/CLT (AA), JetBlue JFK, United EWR. Door-to-door ~10–12h. Backup STI (Santiago) ~1.5–2h; the Amber Highway (Santiago–Puerto Plata) under construction will cut Cibao→coast to ~35–45 min ~est.~ [flag — verify].

**Prices:** Cabarete median asking (Jul 2026): $304.5K condos / $482.5K villas; entry condos from ~$100K, houses from $150K; Sosúa from ~$200K. Cheapest credible beach-town pricing in this screen; steady single-digit gains lagging Las Terrenas/Punta Cana. Encuentro = surf ridge, gated villa/land, best new-build momentum; Kite Beach/Cabarete Bay = condo STR core; Sosúa = cheapest but decades-old sex-tourism reputation caps the brand; Cabrera = luxury-frontier oceanfront bluffs 1h east, no airlift, pure land-bank (~$150–300/sqm ~est.~).

**Rentals:** ADR $108–172, occupancy 33.5–52%, avg revenue $13–19K, RevPAR ~$60, 1,027 listings. ~4–6% gross on a $304K median condo — mediocre; wind-sport demand niche/seasonal.

**STR/tax/title/financing:** Same DR framework (unregulated STRs, CONFOTUR, deslinde, cash-dominant).

**Safety:** Large expat communities, crime below urban DR; Sosúa reputational more than violent. Level 2.

**Momentum:** **Punta Bergantín** — state-led flagship resort/tech-tourism megaproject between POP and Puerto Plata, active through 2026, ~11,000 direct jobs projected; Dominican Today ties tax reform + tourism records + the resort to North Coast RE growth. Amber Highway + possible POP airlift growth = the catalyst stack. Cruise traffic (Taino Bay/Amber Cove) already revived Puerto Plata.

**Supply:** Weak in Cabarete/Sosúa — ample land; appreciation depends on catalysts, not scarcity. Cabrera bluffs = the scarcity play, poor exit liquidity.

**Hazards:** North coast fewer direct hits historically; **Septentrional fault runs directly through this coast** (2003 M6.4 Puerto Plata); sargassum episodic on windward beaches.

**Bull:** Cheapest entry on the board sitting one ridge from a state-sponsored megaproject and a highway that will fuse it to Santiago's metro — if the North Coast re-rates, the % move is biggest here. **Bear:** 30 years of "Cabarete is next" hasn't happened: 33–52% occupancy, $60 RevPAR, no scarcity, Sosúa's brand problem, a fault underfoot — a momentum-free value trap until POP airlift shows up.
**Scores:** Access 5 | Value 8 | Momentum 5 | Catalysts 7 | Risk 5 → **30**
