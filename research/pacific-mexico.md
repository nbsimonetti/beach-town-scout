## 1. San Pancho (San Francisco, Nayarit) + Lo de Marcos + Chacala — Riviera Nayarit north corridor

**Access:** DFW–PVR nonstop excellent — AA only, ~12–15 weekly (~2x daily), ~2h47m. PVR→San Pancho ~45–50 min; →Lo de Marcos ~55–60; →Chacala ~1h20–1h30. Door-to-door DFW→San Pancho ~5.5 hrs — genuinely 30A-like logistics. Secondary: Tepic Riviera Nayarit Intl upgraded 2023–24 (mostly domestic so far). New Compostela/Las Varas–Bucerías autopista segments cut GDL→corridor to ~3–3.5 hrs [KB].

**Prices:** San Pancho average list ~$1.4M at ~$411/sq ft (~$4,400/m²); condo presales $518K–$1.4M; villas/beachfront $2.49M–$8.5M. San Pancho now prices at or above Sayulita. **Lo de Marcos = the value spillover:** town lots $70K–$275K; near-beach $195K–$240K; homes $250K–$600K. **Chacala:** thin; villas $300K–$900K, ocean-view lots $60K–$150K [KB]. Corridor roughly doubled 2019–2024 on Sayulita overflow [KB, broker-sourced].

**Rentals:** Sayulita proxy (AirROI 2026): 35.7% occupancy, $260 ADR, ~$23,952 avg annual revenue, RevPAR $92, 1,553 listings, supply +12.9% y/y. Implied gross yield on $700K–$1M San Pancho villa ~3–5% — appreciation play, not yield. Bahía de Banderas is Mexico's #4 Airbnb market.

**STR reg:** Nayarit lodging tax (~3–5%, confirm); federal ISR/IVA platform withholding; no municipal permit regime or caps as of 2025; tightening expected. Risk LOW currently.

**Title:** Restricted zone → fideicomiso. Parts of Lo de Marcos remain ejido (1936 reform); possessory-rights purchases insecure. Chacala historically an ejido village; verify PROCEDE-regularized title parcel-by-parcel. San Pancho core mostly regularized [KB].

**Financing:** Cash-dominant (~90%+); MoXi-type ~9–12% APR, 35–50% down; developer presale terms common.

**Safety:** Nayarit Level 2 — best of the four corridors. Tourist-corridor violence rare; CJNG treats the tourism economy as protected revenue [KB].

**Infrastructure:** Highway catalyst already landed. San Pancho recurring dry-season water stress; growth outpacing treatment plant [KB].

**Momentum/supply:** Mandarina anchor — One&Only (2020) + Rosewood Mandarina (2025) ~20 min north; Costa Canuva (Fairmont/Ritz pipeline) near Compostela [KB]. San Pancho hemmed by hills, estuary/turtle reserve, and anti-overdevelopment community — genuine supply constraint; Sayulita saturation pushes buyers north (the 30A "next beach town over" dynamic).

**Hazards:** Hurricane real — Roslyn (Oct 2022, Cat 3) landfall almost exactly here; Lidia (2023, Cat 4) just south. Active subduction coast (moderate seismic). No sargassum.

**Bull:** The literal next town past Sayulita with 30A-grade DFW logistics, luxury anchors compressing land values southward, hard supply constraints. **Bear:** No longer early — $411/sq ft prices in the story; thin yields; Roslyn-type hit + water strain underpriced; Lo de Marcos/Chacala ejido landmines.
**Scores:** Access 9 | Value 4 (Lo de Marcos 6) | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 7 → **33**

## 2. Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca (+ Huatulco comparator)

**Access:** DFW–PXM nonstop EXISTS as of Dec 3, 2025 — AA, year-round, ~1x weekly (Sat), ~3h05m. Otherwise one-stop via IAH (United daily-ish, launched Apr 2025 — PXM's first international route) or via MEX/GDL. WestJet Calgary + Air Canada Toronto seasonal 2025. Airport→La Punta/Zicatela 10–15 min. Door-to-door ~4.5 hrs on the nonstop day. Huatulco comparator: DFW–HUX nonstop, AA, 2x weekly.

**Prices — the dramatic one:** ~111–160% cumulative 5-yr appreciation: early-2020 ~$180K properties now $380–420K prime (+111–133%); beachfront lots ~$2,000/m² (2020) → $4,500–5,200/m² (2025) (+125–160%) (broker-published, promotional-adjacent but directionally corroborated). Recent pace: Zicatela +18–22% (2024–25); La Punta premium +20–25% (2025). Entry stack: raw land developing zones $30–100/m²; La Punta condos $150K–$350K; villas $350K–$900K. Still 40–60% below equivalent Sayulita/San Pancho product. Huatulco: FONATUR master-planned, cheaper, slower, cleaner title, Canadian-retiree profile.

**Rentals:** 1,218–1,223 listings; occupancy 35.9% (one source 56%; use ~36–45%); ADR ~$167; avg annual revenue ~$15,315; RevPAR $58; **supply +32.2% y/y**. On a $250K La Punta condo: 6–10% gross — best yield math of the four corridors, but supply growth compressing fast. Extreme seasonality.

**STR/politics:** Oaxaca lodging tax ~3% [KB]; no formal permit regime yet. **Political risk REAL and rising:** organized anti-gentrification movement — 2025 forums demanding moratorium on new tourist-accommodation licenses and strict Airbnb regulation; CDMX's 2024–25 regulation wave is the template.

**Title — THE core risk:** Restricted zone → fideicomiso, but the bigger issue is AGRARIAN: Puerto Escondido sits across comunal lands of San Pedro Mixtepec and Santa María Colotepec. Individual "sales" of comunal parcels can be contested by the assembly or invalidated by agrarian tribunals; "titles from Juquila" not legally binding; foreigners cannot own comunal land at all. A large share of "cheap land" inventory is exactly this. **Only buy regularized private title with title insurance; assume a meaningful chunk of the market is un-financeable and un-insurable.**

**Safety:** Oaxaca Level 2. Coast low-cartel; risks are petty theft, road safety, occasional social/political blockades.

**Infrastructure:** Highway catalyst landed Feb 4, 2024 — Barranca Larga–Ventanilla autopista cut Oaxaca City→coast 6.5 → ~2.5 hrs. Airport catalyst underway: ~$200M terminal expansion targeting 1.3M pax by 2027 → 2.5M by 2037. **Utilities the binding constraint:** pop 30K (2020)→~47K (2025); tourism +145% 2021–22; untreated sewage to sea, water shortages, overflowing landfill, threats to the Zicatela wave itself — Tulum-2016 déjà vu.

**Hazards:** Highest seismic of the four (Oaxaca subduction; M7.4 Jun 2020 at Huatulco ~100 km); tsunami exposure beachfront. Hurricane fewer direct hits historically (Agatha 2022 just east). No sargassum.

**Bull:** Only market with both a fresh DFW nonstop and 100%+ trailing 5-yr appreciation, plus two hard catalysts (2024 highway, $200M airport) still compounding — and 6–10% gross yields. **Bear:** You can lose the whole asset to an agrarian tribunal; sewage/water crisis + anti-gentrification politics could cap the boom; 32% annual STR supply growth eroding yields.
**Scores:** Access 6 | Value 6 | Momentum 9 | Catalysts 9 | Risk 4 → **34** · Huatulco: 6/6/4/5/7 → 28

## 3. Barra de Navidad / Melaque / Costalegre corridor, Jalisco

**Access:** Weakest — but AA announced DFW–ZLO (Manzanillo–Costalegre) nonstop starting April 2026 per route trackers [single-source; verify launched — likely seasonal]. ZLO→Barra ~30–40 min. Otherwise Alaska LAX–ZLO or DFW–PVR + ~3.5–4 hr drive. Chalacatepec/Costalegre Airport (Tomatlán): state-built, federal backing, authorization phase early 2025, Jalisco's 3rd international airport just north of Xala, ~1h15 north of Barra — timeline has slipped repeatedly; treat as 2026–28 catalyst.

**Prices:** Data-thin FSBO/small-broker market (itself an earliness signal). Barra in-town $150K–$400K; canal-front with docks $400K–$900K; Isla Navidad premium $500K–$2M+; Melaque $120K–$350K; large beachfront raw land ~$43/m². Probably the cheapest per-m² beach product of the four corridors — maybe +30–50% over 5 yrs [KB estimate].

**Rentals:** No AirDNA-grade coverage; Canadian/snowbird Nov–Apr; ADR ~$80–150; gross 4–6% [KB low-confidence]. Not a cash-flow market yet.

**STR reg:** Jalisco lodging tax now 5%; no dedicated STR permit in most municipalities; a statewide 180-night annual cap pending in Jalisco Congress as of 2026 — watch.

**Title:** Barra/Melaque cores largely regularized with decades of foreign ownership; Costalegre raw stretches include ejido (Tomatlán/La Huerta); Xala assembled land with agrarian history — buy inside master developments or long-held private title only [KB].

**Safety:** Jalisco Level 3 (CJNG home state) with carve-outs; Costalegre reality: CJNG monopoly = low visible conflict; Colima (Manzanillo airport, 20 min from Barra) is Level 4 — you fly into a Level 4 state's airport to reach it. Statistically calm for tourists; headline/advisory drag real.

**Momentum — the institutional bet:** Costalegre branded as Mexico's largest luxury tourism development pole, $1B+ committed, 20-year state push. Anchors: Four Seasons Tamarindo (2022), Xala (3,000-acre master plan: Six Senses Xala hotel + branded residences opening ~2026–27), Montage/Ritz names circling. Supply constraints deliberate — low-density ethos, huge land held by few families/funds; Barra/Melaque geographically boxed (lagoon/hills).

**Hazards:** Highest hurricane hit-rate of the four (Jova 2011 ground zero; Patricia 2015 landfall at Cuixmala; Lidia 2023 just north). Severe seismic (1932 M8.2 Jalisco — Mexico's largest; 1995 M8 Manzanillo; both tsunamigenic).

**Bull:** Cheapest land basis of the four directly between two of the world's most heavily capitalized luxury corridors with two airport catalysts about to compress a 4-hr drive into 30 minutes. **Bear:** The catalysts are promises — Chalacatepec has slipped a decade, DFW–ZLO unproven — and until they land you own an illiquid FSBO asset in a Level 3 state next to a Level 4 state, on Mexico's most hurricane/tsunami-hit coastline.
**Scores:** Access 4 (→7 if airports land) | Value 8 | Momentum 5 | Catalysts 8 | Risk 5 → **30**

## 4. Mazatlán, Sinaloa — screened out for now

DFW–MZT nonstop thin (1–2x weekly, cut back post-2024 violence). Cerritos ~$288/sq ft, avg condo ~$230K; 91% of pipeline is condos. Airbtics: 36% occupancy, ADR ~$91, ~$11K/yr, 5,376 listings — "a risky market." **Sinaloa: US State Dept Level 4 — DO NOT TRAVEL.** Chapitos vs La Mayiza war since Sept 2024: 2,197 homicides + 2,133 kidnappings statewide in year one; Mazatlán itself 95 homicides Jan–Sep 2025 (+227%), tourism down 25–30%, hotel layoffs, highway blockades. Level 4 poisons practicalities (insurers/lenders/employers restrict travel; resale pool shrinks).
**Bull:** liquid clean-title $3,100/m² beachfront at a fear discount if the war resolves. **Bear:** Level 4 mid-cartel-war, no visible resolution — dead money with personal-safety and liquidity risk.
**Scores:** Access 5 | Value 7 | Momentum 2 | Catalysts 3 | Risk 2 → **19**
