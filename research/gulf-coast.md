# Gulf Coast "Next 30A" Screen — Non-Texas (Aug 2026)

**Dallas travel baseline:** AA nonstops DFW→PNS (Pensacola), DFW→ECP (Panama City), DFW→GPT (Gulfport, 18x/week ~2h09m), DFW→Mobile. Southwest DAL→PNS and DAL→ECP. Mobile commercial service relocating to new downtown BFM terminal (opened late 2025/early 2026, ~21 weekly flights incl. Dallas). ECP record 2025: 1,937,244 pax, +3.1% YoY, North Terminal expansion underway.

**Florida insurance context (all FL towns):** first stabilization in a decade — Citizens announced −8.7% avg statewide rate cut for 2026; 546,000+ policies depopulated to private carriers in 2025. But Gulf-exposed Panhandle counties see smaller cuts; Gulf-front homes still commonly $10K–$20K+/yr, plus flood.

## 1. Bay St. Louis, MS (Hancock Co.)
- **Travel:** Best-in-class. ~7.5 hr / ~500 mi drive from Dallas, or DFW→GPT nonstop + ~30 min. Drivable weekend = unique in this screen.
- **Prices:** Zillow typical $230,250, +6.3% YoY; median sold ~$343K (Jun 2026); beachfront/Old Town $500K–$1M+ — a fraction of any FL analog.
- **Inventory:** DOM 94–111 days; market cooling, buyers negotiating.
- **Rentals:** 289 active STRs, avg revenue $30.7K/yr, 35% occupancy, $275 ADR; supply +35.1% YoY yet ADR/revenue still rose (AirROI).
- **STR regs:** Registration since Jan 2025; $100/yr, no cap — legitimizing, not restricting.
- **Insurance/risk:** Katrina ground zero (2005, ~28–30 ft surge). MS wind pool materially cheaper than FL; much stock already elevated post-Katrina.
- **Momentum:** USA Today "coolest coastal towns" nod; Depot District buildout, new restaurants, art walks; 2025 press: "experience it before it becomes everyone's favorite beach escape."
- **Supply:** Moderate — small historic core finite, inland Hancock County wide open.
- **Character:** Artsy small town moving upscale-casual — closest to "Seaside 1990" energy in the screen, but on brown-water MS Sound.
- **Bull:** Cheapest entry with a genuine walkable arts downtown, real press momentum, drivable, no insurance crisis. **Bear:** It's the Mississippi Sound — no white sand/clear water — which historically caps the luxury ceiling that made 30A.
- **Scores:** Access 9 | Value 9 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 5 → **36**

## 2. Pass Christian, MS
- ~7.5 hr drive; GPT ~20 min. Median list $399K–$484K; Scenic Drive waterfront $1M+; PPSF −7% YoY. STRs allowed with significant controls (most restrictive of MS trio). Old-money enclave, thin commercial core, no boutique-hotel wave.
- **Bull:** Prestige waterfront at 1/5th FL pricing. **Bear:** negative PPSF, STR-restrictive, sleepy — preservation market, not boom.
- **Scores:** Access 9 | Value 6 | Momentum 4 | Catalysts 3 | Risk 5 → **27**

## 3. Ocean Springs, MS
- ~8 hr drive; GPT ~25 min. Zillow avg $275,246, +0.5% YoY; DOM 67 (vs 126 a year ago — fastest-moving MS coast market). Best food/arts scene on MS coast (Walter Anderson legacy).
- **Bull:** Real cultural downtown + lowest entry + fast DOM. **Bear:** not a beachfront market (harbor/sound frontage) — lifestyle-town play, not "next 30A."
- **Scores:** Access 8 | Value 8 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 6 → **33**

## 4. Dauphin Island, AL
- ~9.5 hr drive, or DFW→Mobile + ~50 min. Median whipsawing: $528.5K (May 2025, +22.9%) → $495K (−6%) by 2026; DOM 109; inventory building. ~924 listings; ADR $342–$401; occupancy 35.6–64% by source; avg revenue ~$39.4K/yr.
- **Insurance/risk — the story:** Most expensive homeowners insurance town in Alabama, avg $13,863/yr. West-end erosion up to −4.7 m/yr; mayor publicly warning about the island's long-term existence; West End restoration not until early 2027.
- **Bull:** BFM airport upgrade + cheapest true white-sand AL barrier island frontage. **Bear:** eroding island, $14K/yr insurance, mayor questions its existence — appreciation thesis structurally broken on the west end.
- **Scores:** Access 6 | Value 5 | Momentum 3 | Catalysts 3 | Risk 2 → **19**

## 5. Fort Morgan, AL (unincorp. Baldwin Co.)
- ~10 hr drive, or DFW→PNS/Mobile + ~1h45m–2h (dead-end Hwy 180). ~312 listings, avg list $742,250. AL Gulf Coast record $923M lodging spend 2025; Gulf Shores metro 9,833 listings, $45.8K avg revenue, 57% occupancy; Fort Morgan beachfront weekly rates $3K–$10K+, peak occupancy 90%+ managed. Unincorporated = permissive STR. Bon Secour NWR blocks large stretches — genuine supply constraint; single road; no commercial core possible. Sally (2020) direct hit.
- **Bull:** Permissive-STR, refuge-constrained supply next to a record-tourism engine. **Bear:** 45-min dead-end road, zero town formation — can appreciate, can never BECOME a 30A.
- **Scores:** Access 6 | Value 5 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 4 → **26**

## 6. Perdido Key, FL (Escambia Co.)
- DFW→PNS or DAL→PNS + ~45 min; ~10 hr drive. Median sale $707K, +1.1% YoY; DOM 70. Condo-dominated: Vista Del Mar = first new condo completed since 2007; Meridian (81 ultra-lux) preconstruction; Lennar's Lost Key adding townhomes/villas + beachfront homes + 5,000 sf beach club.
- **Rentals:** 166 listings, $34.6K avg revenue, $378 ADR, 35.3% occupancy, revenue +7.4% YoY; 89% professionally managed.
- **STR regs:** Low-regulation (FL preemption; registration/TDT only).
- **Supply constraint — best in class:** Gulf Islands National Seashore (east end) + Perdido Key State Park mid-island + beach mouse Habitat Conservation Plan caps developable parcels.
- **Risk:** Sally flooding; FL condo crisis drag (statewide condo prices −7%; milestone-inspection/reserve assessments).
- **Bull:** Hardest supply cap on the Gulf + first new-construction cycle in 18 years just starting + white sand 45 min from a two-airline Dallas nonstop. **Bear:** condo-heavy amid FL condo-fee recession, flat momentum.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 5 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 7 | Risk 4 → **29**

## 7. Navarre Beach, FL (Santa Rosa Co.)
- DFW/DAL→PNS + ~45 min, or VPS + ~40 min. Small-sample volatile: median $740K → $865K → ~$910K (2026). Mainland Navarre far cheaper (~$350Ks).
- **CRITICAL STRUCTURE:** All Navarre Beach property is LEASEHOLD — Escambia County owns the island, 99-year lease to Santa Rosa County from 1956, sub-leased (~$250/yr) plus property taxes. Lease horizon (~2055) will increasingly bite financing/exit math.
- **Rentals — best in screen:** $85K median annual revenue, 67% occupancy, $342 ADR, 874 listings (Airbtics Feb'25–Jan'26).
- **Supply:** Gulf Islands National Seashore hems the beach on BOTH sides — developed strip can never extend. Low-rise limits.
- **Bull:** Best rental yield on the non-TX Gulf inside a hard supply cap. **Bear:** you never own the land — a 99-year lease past its halfway point is a slow-motion valuation problem 30A never had.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 5 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 4 → **29**

## 8. Mexico Beach, FL (Bay Co.)
- DFW→ECP or DAL→ECP + ~40 min — closest Forgotten Coast point to a Dallas nonstop; ~11.5 hr drive. Median list mid-$400s to mid-$500s (H1 2026); essentially all stock post-2018, built to current code and elevated.
- **Rentals:** 52% occupancy, $439 ADR, ~$37K annual revenue (AirDNA).
- **STR regs:** Permissive — DBPR license + Bay County certificate + TDT.
- **Risk:** Michael (2018, Cat 5) ground zero — surge to rooflines; ~80% of Panhandle flood losses were uninsured; Citizens buyers effectively forced into flood coverage.
- **Momentum/character:** Deliberate low-density rebuild (height caps, no chains) preserving Old Florida — plus the ECP growth engine 40 min away. Population/tax base still below 2018.
- **Bull:** Entirely new code-built elevated stock at half of Cape San Blas pricing, 40 min from a growing Southwest airport. **Bear:** literal roofline-surge precedent; mandatory flood + wind stack makes carry cost the whole story.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 7 | Momentum 5 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 3 → **28**

## 9. Port St. Joe / Cape San Blas / St. Joe Beach, FL (Gulf Co.) — top "next 30A" analog
- ECP + ~50–75 min; ~12 hr drive. (Gulf Co. splits ET/CT.)
- **Prices:** Port St. Joe median sold $440K, +2.3% YoY; PPSF $268, +9.2%. Cape San Blas median list $1.12M; +4.28% YoY (Nov 2025); 2025: 97 sales vs 76 in 2024, volume +15% to $100.3M — demand re-accelerating.
- **Rentals:** PSJ market 843 listings, $44.6K avg revenue, $502 ADR, 35.6% occupancy — but supply +55% YoY; Cape San Blas subset $567 ADR, ~$38.6K; premium Gulf-front homes grossing $200K+/yr.
- **STR regs:** Gulf County business license — registration, not restriction.
- **THE CATALYST — St. Joe Co.:** JOE owns 165,000 acres, ~87% in Bay/Gulf/Walton counties, ~90% within 15 mi of the Gulf; entitlements for 170K+ units / 22M sf commercial / 3,000+ hotel rooms in the Bay-Walton Sector Plan; operating Point South Marina Port St. Joe (427 slips w/ Bay Point); fighting the county over annexation for future development (2025); Eastern Shipbuilding broke ground on the old paper-mill site (jobs base). Same landowner/playbook that manufactured WaterColor/Watersound and the 30A boom — Gulf County is its next frontier. (SEC 10-K)
- **Supply:** St. Joseph Peninsula State Park caps the Cape's north half; conservation setbacks keep density low.
- **Risk:** Michael damage (less than Mexico Beach); documented severe historical erosion on the Cape (repeated renourishment); FL insurance stack.
- **Bull:** The only market where the CREATOR of 30A is the dominant landowner with marina/hospitality already in the ground — purest structural "next 30A" bet, PPSF still +9%. **Bear:** STR supply +55% in one year crushing per-listing yields; JOE's timeline contested and slow (annexation fight).
- **Scores:** Access 6 | Value 6 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 9 | Risk 4 → **32**

## 10. St. George Island / Apalachicola / Carrabelle, FL (Franklin Co.)
- Worst FL-Panhandle access: ECP ~2h; ~12.5 hr drive. SGI median sale $1.2M–$1.42M; list −7% YoY; DOM 134. Rentals: 170 listings, $54.2K avg revenue, $520 ADR, 40.2% occupancy — supply +58.9% YoY. Excellent supply constraint (9-mile state park, preserve, low-rise, no chains). Apalachicola historic homes ~$400–600Ks; oyster-collapse hurt working waterfront.
- **Bull:** Highest STR revenue on the Forgotten Coast with genuine scarcity. **Bear:** at $1.2M+ median, softening prices, 134 DOM — already discovered; worst Dallas logistics in the Panhandle.
- **Scores:** Access 4 | Value 4 | Momentum 4 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 4 → **21**
