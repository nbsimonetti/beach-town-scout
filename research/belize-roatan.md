# Western Caribbean / Central America "Next Tulum" Screen — Belize, Roatán, Wildcards (Aug 2026)

*~85% web-sourced; remainder flagged [EST]. Belize pricing sources are heavily realtor-published — appreciation claims discounted as promotional-biased.*

## 1. Belize — Ambergris Caye (San Pedro + Secret Beach)

**Access:** AA is the only nonstop DFW–BZE, 6–8x weekly, ~3h. Then Tropic Air/Maya Island BZE→SPR ~20 min (~$90–125) or ferry ~90 min. **Door-to-door DFW→San Pedro ~6–7 hrs — best-in-class for the region.** Secret Beach adds 30–45 min golf-cart/rough road.

**Prices:** Entry 1BR inland condo $200–280K; mid 2BR beach-adjacent $420–600K; premium 2–3BR beachfront $900K–$1.5M+; beachfront homes $500K–$5M; inland lots $75–180K, beachfront lots $200K–$2M. Realtors claim +30% $/sf 2024 and 9–14% YoY hotspots; longer-run credible figure 5–8%/yr for a decade on beachfront. No MLS, thin comps — quoted appreciation is ask-price-driven.

**Secret Beach honest take:** Shifting from land speculation to income development (first lumberyard 2025), but thousands of platted lots, near-zero resale liquidity, heavy 10%-down seller-financing marketing — classic lot-speculation structure. **Treat Secret Beach lots as venture-style, not appreciation-with-liquidity.** Built/income product near the beach bars is the defensible play. Secret Beach is on the west (lagoon) side = no sargassum — its one structural advantage.

**Rentals:** Belize national STR occupancy 68% (2024, +10.9% YoY), ~71% early 2025. Gross yields 5–8% beachfront; honest net math: $500K beachfront 2BR grossing ~$35K → net 2–3% after 20–30% mgmt, HOA, insurance. ADR ~$250–400 beachfront 2BR [EST].

**STR regulation:** BTB registration/license before listing, annual renewal, 9% accommodation tax remitted monthly; fines to BZ$10K; BTB-Airbnb partnership finds unlicensed listings. Mild friction, not a crackdown risk.

**Ownership:** Fee simple, no restrictions, English common law, contracts in English. Stamp duty 8% foreign (5% citizens); all-in closing 10–12%. Quirks: dual/triple title systems, historical Lands Department fraud/duplicate-title scandals — use title insurance (available) + established attorney.

**Financing:** Mostly cash; Belize bank mortgages ~10–12% [EST]; pervasive seller financing on Secret Beach lots is itself a soft-demand tell.

**Safety:** Belize Level 2 [EST]; cayes materially safer than Belize City; San Pedro has had gang-linked shootings 2024–25 [EST]; tourist-targeted violence rare.

**Infrastructure:** North-road paving progressing; Secret Beach water/power the binding constraint (trucked water/cisterns, partial grid) [EST]. PGIA expansion announced [EST — verify].

**Momentum:** Alaia (Marriott Autograph) open 2021, trading well; Six Senses Belize under construction; Four Seasons Caye Chapel still effectively stalled (no verified 2025–26 milestone). Tourism +30% 2024 claim (RE/MAX). Real but discount branded-pipeline headlines.

**Supply/hazards:** True beachfront on developed south/mid island genuinely scarce; Secret Beach lots abundant. Hurricanes less frequent than Yucatán/US Gulf but not immune (Hattie 1961, Lisa 2022). Reef side gets sargassum (2025 record year [EST]); reef blunts but doesn't eliminate.

**Bull:** The only Caribbean island 3 hrs nonstop from DFW with fee-simple English-law title and structurally growing US tourism; beachfront scarcity + branded pipeline compounding 5–8%+. **Bear:** Buying after the 2021–24 run at US-coastal prices with 2–3% net yields, no MLS transparency, and a Secret Beach lot machine that will saturate resale supply for years.
**Scores:** Access 9 | Value 5 | Momentum 8 | Catalysts 7 | Risk 6 → **35** (Secret Beach lots only: 7/4/6/5/3 → 25)

## 2. Belize — Placencia

DFW–BZE + Tropic/Maya hop ~35–45 min (~$130–160 [EST]) or 3.5–4 hr drive. Door-to-door ~7–8 hrs. 2BR condos $250–450K; beachfront homes $450K–$1.2M+. ~35% cumulative appreciation "last few years" claimed (realtor source). Occupancy ~55–65%, ADR $200–350 [EST]; gross 5–8%, net 2–4%. Same Belize framework (BTB + 9%; 8% stamp). Peninsula road paved; Coastal Highway (2022) cut Belize City→south ~1 hr. Itz'ana open; no verified major new international flag under construction [EST]. Narrow 16-mile peninsula = genuine beach-side scarcity. **Hurricane Iris (2001, Cat 4) made landfall directly here and flattened the peninsula** — highest hurricane-scar of the Belize trio; low elevation, surge-exposed.
**Bull:** Belize's best actual beach + walkable village at 30–40% discount to San Pedro, Coastal Highway rerouting growth south. **Bear:** two-leg access, Iris-class bullseye on a 500m-wide sand spit, no catalyst big enough to force repricing.
**Scores:** Access 6 | Value 6 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 5 → **28**

## 3. Belize — Hopkins

DFW–BZE + ~2–2.5 hr drive (road transfer, no hop). Door-to-door ~6.5–7.5 hrs. 2–3BR homes $140–280K; **true beachfront $250–550K — the cheapest beachfront in this screen.** Lots $30–50K (2019) ask $80–150K now [EST]. Shallow STR market: occ 45–60%, ADR $150–275 [EST]; gross can screen 7–10% on low bases. Coastal Highway + paved access road took Hopkins from 4-hr slog to 2-hr drive. Garifuna cultural cachet, boutique resorts (Hamanasi). No branded pipeline, no airport, no marina — no catalyst forcing appreciation on a schedule. Long beach, abundant flat land behind it — supply NOT constrained. Hurricane Nana (2020, Cat 1) landfall essentially at Hopkins.
**Bull:** Cheapest credible beachfront in the region 3 hrs + a paved drive from DFW; if Belize's south is the next corridor, Hopkins is pre-institutional entry at 2019 Placencia prices. **Bear:** No supply constraint, no flag, no airstrip — "emerging" can stay emerging 15 years; exit buyer pool is a handful of expats.
**Scores:** Access 6 | Value 8 | Momentum 5 | Catalysts 4 | Risk 5 → **28**

## 4. Roatán, Honduras

**Access:** AA DFW–RTB nonstop seasonal-but-nearly-year-round (Sat only Oct–Nov; Wed+Sat Dec–Apr and May 24–Aug 31; ~3h15m). Backup United IAH–RTB up to daily peak, AA Miami daily, Delta ATL. Door-to-door ~5–6 hrs on nonstop days, else ~7–8 via IAH. RTB terminal renovation ongoing [EST].

**Prices:** Avg condo $325K, avg home $397K island-wide; 1BR ~$228K, 2BR ~$323K; West Bay condos from ~$250K. 5-yr CAGR ~5% island-wide; West Bay/West End 6–10% in 12 months to Jan 2026; forecast 3–7%/yr to 2030 (TheLatinvestor). West Bay/West End = walkable beach + dive economy, deepest STR demand. East end = golf-resort/land plays, thinner resale; Próspera overhang at Crawfish Rock.

**Rentals:** Well-located 5–8% gross; ADR ~$150–300, occ ~55–65% [EST]. Nuance: 1M+ cruise passengers don't sleep ashore — stayover air capacity is the real STR driver, and it's inflecting. **Non-resident rental income: ~25% withholding on gross** unless structured through a Honduran corp — materially worse than Belize.

**STR regulation:** Effectively no licensing regime; 4% tourism tax [EST]; condo-document rental restrictions are the practical constraint.

**Ownership (honest):** Constitution Art. 107 bars foreign ownership near coasts; Decree 90-90 carve-out: a foreign individual may directly title ONE urban residential property ≤3,000 m². Bigger/multiple/commercial → Honduran SA corporation (standard, cheap, annual compliance; you own shares, not dirt). Real risks: weak folio real records, family/possession claims, cadastral mismatches, historic double-titling — 10–20 yr title chain review + limited title insurance essential. Closing 3–6%; 45–120 days to registered title; property tax ~$500–1,500/yr.

**ZEDE/Próspera saga:** ZEDE law repealed Apr 2022; Supreme Court declared ZEDEs unconstitutional retroactively Sept 2024; Próspera still operating under claimed 50-yr stability agreement while pressing a $10.7B ICSID claim (~1/3 of Honduran GDP); Honduras withdrew from ICSID Aug 2024. Read-through for a West Bay condo buyer: near-zero legally, but it advertises rule-of-law volatility and keeps institutional capital cautious — partly why Roatán trades at half of Ambergris.

**Financing:** Cash dominant; Honduran banks 50–70% LTV at 10–14% (effectively unusable); seller/developer financing 6–9%/5–10 yrs [EST].

**Safety:** Honduras Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" with Bay Islands explicitly noted as lower-crime [EST — verify current text]. On-island crime well below mainland.

**Infrastructure:** RTB upgrades; roads rough off the West corridor; RECO power ~$0.35–0.45/kWh, among the Caribbean's priciest, with outages — budget solar [EST]; water at capacity in dry season.

**Hazards:** No direct major hurricane hit since Mitch (1998) — low-latitude track advantage, not immunity; Swan Islands transform fault just north (M7.3 2009, M7.6 2018 offshore); **sargassum largely spared on north/west-facing beaches (West Bay faces NW) — a genuine edge over Riviera Maya/Belize east coasts.**

**Bull:** Half the price of Ambergris for a better beach and better diving, with airlift compounding and West Bay supply capped — classic pre-repricing setup. **Bear:** Honduran sovereign/institutional risk (Art. 107 workarounds, 25% withholding, ZEDE fights, Level 3 headline) may permanently cap the exit multiple; power costs and title archaeology eat the carry.
**Scores:** Access 7 | Value 8 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 4 → **32**

## 5. Wildcards (screened briefly)

- **Isla Mujeres, MX — best wildcard.** DFW–CUN ~10+ dailies + 20-min Ultramar ferry — best true door-to-door in the entire international screen (~5 hrs) [EST]. Condos $250–500K, strong ADRs off Cancún's airport, structurally supply-capped 5-mile island. Costs: fideicomiso, QRoo lodging tax + new state STR registry, sargassum on east shore, Tulum-saturation spillover risk. "Already-Tulum-adjacent" rather than "next Tulum" — but for a DFW buyer wanting liquidity + access it beats everything Belizean on exit depth. **Scores:** 10/5/7/6/6 → **34**
- **Costa Rica — Playa Negra/Marbella (Nosara overflow) — qualified pass.** DFW–LIR nonstop (~3h40m) + 1.5–2 hr drive [EST]. Priced-out-of-Nosara wave real: titled lots $75–200K, homes $350–700K; stable rule of law, no foreign-ownership restrictions, deep gringo resale market. Drags: brutal last-mile roads, water-letter (AyA) constraints, 13% VAT + ICT registration, arguably past discovery phase — wave 3. Surf-led thesis buyers only.
- **Panama (Pedasí/Bocas) — FAIL** (10+ hr door-to-door; Bocas title chaos). **Colombia Caribbean — FAIL** (no DFW nonstops, peso volatility, saturation, security).
