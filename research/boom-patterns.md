### Pre-boom profile (late 1970s–1980s)
- Walton County population: 27,760 (1990) → 40,601 (2000) → 75,305 (2020) → ~93,300 (2025). South Walton in 1980 was scrub pine, "Redneck Riviera" beach shacks; Grayton Beach the only real settlement [GK].
- Land hyper-concentrated: St. Joe Paper Co. owned >1 million acres of Florida at peak; mill sold 1996; ex-Disney Imagineering chairman Pete Rummell hired as CEO 1997 to convert timberland to place-making; still ~171,000 acres, 110,500 under a 50-year Bay-Walton Sector Plan.
- Davis inherited 80 acres (1978), sold 20–30 lots/year, reinvested in infrastructure — deliberate scarcity.

### Catalysts, in sequence
1. 1981–85 Seaside master plan (Duany/Plater-Zyberk) — New Urbanism prototype; design scarcity + walkability = price premium engine.
2. June 1993 Mid-Bay Bridge opens (Destin↔Niceville) cutting mainland access time.
3. 1995–99: Rosemary Beach founded 1995; WaterColor established 1999 by St. Joe (499 acres). VRBO founded 1995 [GK] — 30A a flagship VRBO market pre-Airbnb.
4. 1998 The Truman Show filmed at Seaside — national cultural moment.
5. May 2010: ECP airport opens on 4,000 acres donated by St. Joe; ~1.5M pax by 2022 — unlocked fly-to buyers (Dallas, Houston, Nashville direct).
6. 2020–22 COVID/remote-work + Southern metro wealth surge.

### Demand/supply today
- South Walton: 5M+ visitors, $4.7B direct spend (2023) on 26 miles of coastline.
- ~40% of south Walton is state forest/park [GK — verify %] — buildable beachfront finite, mostly built.

### Appreciation by era (approximate)
1982–1995: lots $15K → low six figures (~10x). 1995–2006: steepest institutional phase. 2007–2011: bust −30–40% [GK]. 2012–2019: recovery/doubling. 2020–2022: +40–60% COVID surge [GK].

## Case Study 2: Outer Banks, NC (Corolla, Duck, Nags Head) — 1980s → today

### Pre-boom profile
- Corolla 1970s: ~15 residents, unpaved, behind a private guard gate; Duck→Corolla road unpaved pre-1985.
- October 1984: state takes over the road → NC-12 extended through Corolla; gate comes down.

### The boom, in homes (Currituck Banks/Corolla)
1984: 422 → 1995: 1,966 → 2000: 2,750. >50% of new homes exceeded 5,000 sq ft — the "event home" (10–24BR wedding/reunion rental) is an OBX invention.

### Prices
- Dare County median sale: $325,000 (2003) → $622,000 (2024) — only ~3.1%/yr across that span (includes the 2006–11 bust); median roughly doubled 2015→2024 (~8%/yr) (Outer Banks Voice 2024).
- 2024 by town: Duck $850K, Southern Shores ~$755K, Kitty Hawk $679K. Q4-2023 all-time quarterly record ($600K median).

### Catalysts
NC-12 takeover 1984; second Wright Memorial Bridge span 1995 [GK]; professionalized weekly-rental industry decades before Airbnb; DC/NoVA/Richmond feeder wealth 4.5–6 hr drive; COVID surge 2020–22. Shape: OBX boomed in VOLUME (homes built) in the 80s–90s and in PRICE mostly 2000–05 and 2020–22.

### Pre-1984 leading indicators
NCDOT road-takeover plans public; developer land held behind the gate; Duck already gentrifying; enormous untouched oceanfront next to a wealthy metro within a tank of gas.

## Case Study 3: Gulf Shores / Orange Beach, AL — 1979/2000s → today

- Gulf Shores population: 3,261 (1990) → 9,741 (2010) → 15,014 (2020, +54.1%/decade).
- Hurricane Frederic (Sept 1979) "leveled most of the town" — insurance-funded rebuild birthed the first condo wall in the 1980s.
- Hurricane Ivan (Sept 2004, Cat 3): developer paid ~$40M for 30 acres/900 front-ft in April 2004 (pre-Ivan), launched Turquoise Place spring 2005 — 24 stories, 173 units averaging $1.5M, closing April 2008. Post-storm high-rises replaced demolished low-rise stock — density up, quality up, insurance-financed. Gold FORTIFIED standards adopted.
- Baldwin County visitors: 5.8M (2020) → 8M+ (2021) → 8.39M (2024).
- Medians (Redfin 2025–26): Gulf Shores ~$452–458K; Orange Beach ~$690K — roughly half of comparable Destin/30A product (the value-play thesis).
- BP spill 2010: tourism recovered within ~a year; settlement money funded public amenity [GK].
- Catalysts: Frederic rebuild → 80s condo wall; Foley Beach Express + toll bridge (2000) [GK — verify]; The Wharf (2006) [GK]; Ivan rebuild; drive-market feeders (Birmingham/Nashville/Memphis/Atlanta/Huntsville); FORTIFIED lowering insurance friction vs FL; price gap vs Florida panhandle.

## Case Study 4: Port Aransas / Texas Coast — 2007/2017 → today

- Population: 1,968 (1980) → 3,370 (2000) → 3,480 (2010) → 2,904 (2020, post-Harvey dip). 2000 median household income $39,432 — a working fishing town.
- Catalysts:
  1. Cinnamon Shore North opens 2007 — first New Urbanism neighborhood on the Texas coast, a deliberate 30A import; survived 2008; Cinnamon Shore South (2019) added 147 acres, ~quadrupling footprint; Palmilla Beach and Sunflower Beach followed.
  2. Hurricane Harvey (Aug 2017) — Cat 4 directly on Port A; ~$1B damage; 85% of homes and 100% of businesses damaged. Rebuild produced newer elevated code-compliant stock; construction-led hot market.
  3. Texas wealth boom feeders — Austin/San Antonio 2.5–3.5 hrs, Houston 3.5–4 hrs, DFW fly/drive; no state income tax; shale + tech wealth [GK].
- Prices: current median sale ~$670K, +8.5% YoY (Redfin ~2026). Pre-boom (~2011–12) median ~$280–320K [estimate — verify via Zillow ZHVI] → ~6–7%/yr CAGR 2012→2026, jumps concentrated post-Harvey (2018–19) and COVID (2020–22). Beachfront new-build in Cinnamon Shore/Palmilla routinely $1.5M–$4M+ [GK].

## Case Study 5A: Hilton Head Island, SC — 1956 → 1980s (the ur-case)

- 1956: first bridge; Charles Fraser founds Sea Pines Company on ~5,000 acres; first lot deed 1958.
- Lot prices: beachfront $5,350 (1958) → $9,600 (1962); half-acre oceanfront ~$5,000 (early 1960s) → $200,000 (1973) — ~28%/yr CAGR over ~12 years.
- The playbook Fraser invented (Seaside/Cinnamon Shore copied): total developer control — master plan, covenants, architecture review; amenity-led (golf, Heritage Classic 1969 [GK]); environment as brand (Sea Pines Forest Preserve).
- Pre-boom: timbering/small farming, no bridge, zero tourism. Copycat cascade: Palmetto Dunes, Hilton Head Plantation, Shipyard, Port Royal, Long Cove, Wexford, Indigo Run.

## Case Study 5B: Cape San Blas / Gulf County, FL — post-Michael 2018 → today

- Hurricane Michael (Oct 2018, Cat 5 at Mexico Beach): ~1,000 homes destroyed/damaged in Port St. Joe; surge cut two new inlets through the cape.
- Key market fact: many owners netted more from insurance payouts than pre-storm market value (e.g., $250K home → $190K combined payouts on a still-owned lot) — capital recycled into bigger rebuilt stock.
- Today: Cape San Blas median sale ~$1.03M, +15% YoY (Homes.com/Redfin ~2025); range $600K–$3M. 30A-overflow pricing driven by the rebuild cycle + priced-out 30A demand. St. George Island runs the same pattern one county east [verify Franklin County median].

| Bolivar Peninsula, TX | Miles of empty sand next to Galveston | Ike 2008 destroyed 62% of all structures (Gilchrist: 99.5%); 21-ft surge on 2-m elevation; pop 3,853→2,417 (2000–10), partial recovery 2,769 (2020); ferry-dependent access; insurance | Wikipedia |
| General kill factors | — | (a) storm frequency > ~1 direct hit/decade WITHOUT offsetting wealth inflow; (b) erosion/subsidence; (c) single fragile access road; (d) industrial adjacency capping amenity value; (e) insurance non-availability post-2022 repricing; (f) absence of a covenant-setting master developer — commodity lots stay commodity | synthesis |

**Key asymmetry:** hurricanes are a CATALYST where demand drivers are intact (Gulf Shores, Port A, Cape San Blas — insurance money + new codes = upgraded stock) and a KILLER where they recur too often against weak demand (Grand Isle, Bolivar). The dividing line is feeder-metro wealth + beach quality + a developer to rebuild to a higher use.

**Rough CAGR league table (nominal):** Sea Pines oceanfront lots 1958–73: ~28%/yr · Seaside lots 1982–2001: ~22%/yr · Seaside lots 1982–2025: ~12–13%/yr · Dare County 2015–24: ~8%/yr · Port Aransas ~2012–26: ~6–7%/yr (est.) · Dare County 2003–24 full span: ~3%/yr (bust included — timing of entry dominates).

**Caveats:** FRED county HPI series (Walton, Dare, Baldwin, Nueces, Gulf) exist and would give clean decade splits — 403-blocked this session.
