# US Non-Gulf Beach Town Screen — "Next 30A / Next OBX" from Dallas (Aug 2026)

**Dallas air-access baseline:** DFW→ILM (AA 2x daily, 2h42m) · DAL→MYR (Southwest) + AA DFW seasonal · EWN/OAJ no Dallas nonstop · SAV yes (AA) · JAX yes (AA + WN DAL) · DAB yes (nonstop DFW) · ORF yes (AA daily) · VRB no Dallas service · SBP only 2x/week AA · SAN/SFO/PDX/SEA multiple dailies.

## Atlantic Coast

### 1. Crystal Coast, NC (Emerald Isle / Atlantic Beach / Beaufort)
No Dallas nonstop within 1h15 — DFW→ILM + 1h20–1h45, real door-to-door ~6h. Emerald Isle median list $875K; Atlantic Beach ~$609K (+3.6%). Oceanfront $1.2–2.5M+. NC structurally STR-friendly. NCIUA wind +16% (2025), +15.9% (2026) hikes. Beaufort "America's Coolest Small Town" press cycle. Closest Atlantic analog to "1995 30A" — but access kills it.
**Scores:** 3/7/7/6/5 → **28**

### 2. Oak Island / Holden Beach / Ocean Isle, NC (Brunswick County)
DFW→ILM + 40–60 min (best NC access). Oak Island median $690K flat, 153 DOM, 7.6-mo supply — real buyer's market. Holden ~$975K–1.1M; OIB oceanfront $2.5–4M+. Oak Island STR ~$42K/yr revenue, ADR ~$465, occ 38–54% (~6% gross). STR-friendly (state-preempted). NCIUA hikes; post-Isaias renourishment special assessments. Brunswick County one of the fastest-growing US counties; ~60–70% 5-yr FHFA appreciation.
**Bull:** best NC combo: ILM nonstop, buyer's-market entry, boom county, STR freedom. **Bear:** sand-poor island with recurring assessments; golf-cart suburbia, not "town."
**Scores:** 6/8/6/7/5 → **32**

### 3. Topsail Island, NC
DFW→ILM + 45–75 min. Topsail Beach avg $966K; Surf City ~$558K (+17.4% noisy). N. Topsail median STR revenue $69K/yr, 57% occ — best NC yields BECAUSE the risk is priced: **N. Topsail is a CBRA zone** (no federal flood insurance/nourishment), chronic severe erosion. South end stable. Surf City growth corridor.
**Scores:** 6/7/6/5/3–5 → **27–29**

### 4. Pawleys Island / Murrells Inlet, SC
DAL→MYR Southwest + 30–45 min — among the best access in the screen. Pawleys ZHVI $549K; island proper $2M+. Murrells median $390–495K, 8.2-mo supply. Permissive STR. Myrtle overflow growth engine. "Next Murrells = current North Myrtle" more than "next 30A" — Myrtle beta, not hidden-gem alpha.
**Scores:** 8/7/6/6/5 → **32**

### 5. Edisto Beach, SC
CHS + 1h30. Median ~$680–719K (−4.1%). Among SC's worst chronic erosion; single road. ACE Basin conservation freeze = scarcity but no catalyst; no airport, no growth engine.
**Scores:** 3/5/4/3/4 → **19**

### 6. Georgia Coast — Tybee / St. Simons / Brunswick–Darien
Tybee: SAV + 30 min; median $730K, DOM 125 vs 66; STR permits frozen/capped in residential zones. St. Simons ZHVI $688,772 (+1.7%) — already the prize. **Brunswick/Darien the actual hidden-gem story:** metro $374.5K, +9% YoY, +72.5% 5-yr FHFA; 900-acre "Jamestown" mixed-use development at Darien; Hyundai Metaplant regional wage injection; Brunswick Old Town gentrifying. Caveats: not itself a beach (marsh gateway); multifamily vacancy 16.6% oversupply warning.
**Scores:** Brunswick/Darien 5/8/8/8/6 → **35** · St. Simons 5/4/5/5/6 → 25 · Tybee 6/4/6/4/5 → 25

### 7. Amelia Island / Fernandina Beach, FL
JAX + 40–50 min (very good). Fernandina median ~$635–780K flat-to-soft; 10-yr appreciation averaged 9.67%/yr. Amelia proper ~$975K–1.075M, DOM 314 (luxury stagnation). **HARD FLAG:** resort rentals restricted to R-3 zones only; 30-day minimum elsewhere. NE FL is Florida's cheapest insurance coast (~$2.2–4K); lowest hurricane landfall frequency in FL. Already institutionally discovered (Ritz, Omni).
**Scores:** 8/5/5/4/7 → **29**

### 8. Flagler Beach, FL
DFW→DAB nonstop + 35 min. Median sale $425K (−13.2% YoY), DOM 51 — cheapest true-character Atlantic FL beach town. STR-legal with city+county certificates (friendlier than Volusia). County insurance ~$4,200 avg, beachside higher; chronic A1A erosion (repeated washouts, Army Corps/seawall projects). Old-Florida low-rise, height-capped, no chains, squeezed between booming Palm Coast (one of FL's fastest-growing cities) and St. Augustine.
**Bull:** Palm Coast growth engine one bridge away + $425K entry + STR-legal + Dallas nonstop 35 min = best pure "next 30A" setup in Florida. **Bear:** −13% YoY print; A1A falls into the ocean every major storm; tiny commercial base.
**Scores:** 7/8/6/7/4 → **32**

### 9. New Smyrna Beach, FL
DAB + 25 min. Median $433–470K (−4 to −15.6%), sales −37% YoY. **HARD FLAG:** STRs only in small designated zones; unincorporated Volusia ban court-upheld; $10K fines. Beachside insurance $6–12K+. This WAS the 2021 "next hidden gem" — already boomed, now in drawdown.
**Scores:** 8/5/3/4/3 → **23**

### 10. Vero Beach / Sebastian, FL
No Dallas path better than one-stop or MCO/PBI + 1h30+. Vero ~$368–410K, 83.6% of listings with price cuts; Sebastian $360K. City fights STRs; county friendlier. Treasure Coast wealth-migration corridor but slow-burn.
**Scores:** 3/7/4/5/5 → **24**

### 11. Chincoteague, VA
ORF + 2h. Median ~$422–499K. Permissive rentals. NASA Wallops/Rocket Lab launch complex next door (space catalyst); 90%+ surrounding land federal refuge = hard scarcity. Very low-lying; among the highest relative sea-level-rise rates on the East Coast.
**Scores:** 2/7/5/6/4 → **24**

### 14. Half Moon Bay ($1.5–1.6M, Bay Area bedroom) → 19. Crescent City (cheap for reasons that don't fix) → 14. **California fails the screen outright.**
### 15. Oregon Coast — Lincoln City / Pacific City / Manzanita
PDX + 1h40–2h20. Lincoln City median $439K–487K. **HARD FLAGS:** Lincoln City residential caps (194 + 91 licenses) BOTH REACHED, applications suspended; Manzanita capped 17.5% of housing, 13-deep waitlist; Tillamook tightened county-wide. Only Lincoln City commercial zones uncapped (condotel loophole). Cascadia tsunami zones on oceanfront. Oregon has 30A's scarcity but legislated away the investor flywheel.
**Scores (Lincoln City):** 4/6/4/3/5 → **22**

### 16. Seabrook, WA — the literal "next Seaside"
SEA + 2h15–2h45. Median sale $984,490, **+17% YoY**; bungalows $700–800K, town-center townhomes $1.3–1.7M. ~600 homes, 20+ shops, still building (founded 2004). STR by design — integrated rental program IS the business model; no cap risk (developer-controlled). Seattle 12-month drive market (4M+ within 3h). Sits on a bluff (vs Ocean Shores at sea level, $375K median, −6 to −7.4% YoY — the premium is brand/planning, not the beach). No hurricane regime; cheap insurance.
**Bull:** only true 30A-mechanics play in the screen — controlled supply release, built-in rental engine, +17% while everything else is flat-down. **Bear:** single-developer risk (exit liquidity = their sales pace); 60°F summers; ~7.5h door-to-door; you're buying year 22 of the story.
**Scores:** 4/4/9/8/7 → **32**

## Great Lakes (brief)
