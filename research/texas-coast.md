# Texas Gulf Coast "Next 30A" Screen — Raw Research Dossier (Aug 2026)

**Method notes:** Prices/DOM from Redfin/Zillow/Movoto (Jan–Aug 2026 snapshots); STR data from AirROI/AirDNA/Rabbu/Airbtics (ranges given). Statewide context: Texas beachfront runs ~40% below Florida equivalents; Gulf Coast beach markets broadly cooled 2024–2026 (inventory up, DOM up, prices flat-to-down) after the 2020–2022 boom — the screen is "who holds value through the correction and has catalysts for the next leg."

**TWIA/insurance macro:** TWIA board voted 0% increase for 2026 after HB 3689 overhauled funding (reinsurance need down ~46%). Avg TWIA residential premium ~$2,541, but coastal beach houses realistically $4,000–$7,000+/yr wind + flood (NFIP RR2.0 V-zone $3K–$10K+) + hazard. Budget 2–4% of home value/yr all-in on beachfront.

## 1. Crystal Beach / Bolivar Peninsula (Galveston Co.)
- **Access:** ~5.5–6.0 hr drive from Dallas (no ferry needed from north); HOU ~1.5 hr.
- **Prices:** Median sale ~$473K; 5-yr (One10 MLS): avg $347,654 (2020) → $618,975 peak (2022) → $474,092 (2025 YTD) = +36% over 5 yrs but −23% off peak. Beachfront new builds $600–900K+.
- **Inventory:** 513 active Jul 2025 vs 297 Jul 2023 (+70%); DOM 291 (Jul 2025).
- **Rentals:** ADR $425, occupancy 34.4%, avg revenue ~$46.7K/yr; supply +24.6% YoY yet revenue rising. ~9.8% gross yield — best on upper coast.
- **STR regs:** Fully permissive — unincorporated, no permit, 6% state HOT only.
- **Risk:** Worst-in-class: Ike (2008) 14-ft surge destroyed most of the peninsula; erosion; NFIP horror stories on non-elevated stock; no Ike Dike yet.
- **Momentum:** Pop +19.8% since 2020; Entergy $110M Bolivar Reliability Project done; 110-acre beachfront resort planned (homes/condos/hotel/airstrip).
- **Supply:** ~27 mi of beachfront, lots of empty lots — NOT constrained (the appreciation governor).
- **Bull:** Cheapest big-inventory STR cash-flow market within 6 hrs of Dallas and Houston. **Bear:** Unlimited lots + extreme storm/insurance exposure caps appreciation; deep buyer's market.
- **Scores:** Access 6 | Value 7 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 2 → **27**

## 2. Surfside Beach (Brazoria Co.)
- ~4.75–5.0 hr — one of the closest true beach towns to Dallas. Median ~$425–430K; DOM 154. Rentals ADR $344, occ 31.5%, ~$30.8K/yr (~7% gross). STR: $200/yr registration. Chronic severe erosion (repeated GLO nourishment + groins); Freeport petrochemical horizon. Genuinely small supply cap between jetties and San Luis Pass.
- **Bull:** Closest-to-Dallas beach with hard supply cap and no-drama STRs. **Bear:** Erosion existential for front row; industrial backdrop caps the luxury ceiling forever — not a 30A candidate aesthetically.
- **Scores:** Access 8 | Value 6 | Momentum 4 | Catalysts 3 | Risk 3 → **24**

## 3. Matagorda (Matagorda Co.)
- ~5.0–5.5 hr. Median list $449K (−9% YoY); beach-tagged median $295K; county median sale $237K. ~95 listings, DOM ~215. No STR vendor data; fishing-driven. Unregulated STRs. Beach off-island across the Intracoastal; much housing riverfront. Quiet; $86M Sargent restoration is the county's flagship spend. High supply constraint (most of peninsula can't be serviced).
- **Bull:** Cheapest entry with genuine scarcity of developable beachfront + unregulated STRs. **Bear:** No growth engine, no airport, thin rental demand — cheap can stay cheap indefinitely.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 8 | Momentum 3 | Catalysts 3 | Risk 5 → **26**

## 4. Sargent (Matagorda Co.)
- ~5.0 hr. Median list ~$395K; sold $320K (−5.2%). Canal-front dominates. Unregulated STRs. **Worst erosion on the Texas coast: 20–43 ft/YEAR** — BUT the $86.3M USACE/GLO Sargent Beach Stabilization is under construction NOW (terminal groin + 5 breakwaters + 1M cu yd nourishment; complete Aug 2027) — first-ever hard defense.
- **Bull:** If the project stabilizes the shoreline, you're buying pre-infrastructure at land-value prices. **Bear:** Betting against 40 ft/yr erosion with one project; effectively uninsurable front row.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 7 | Momentum 3 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 1 → **23**

## 5. Port O'Connor (Calhoun Co.)
- ~6.0 hr. Median list $499K / sale $388K. Bay/canal-front $500K–$1M+. Economy is guided-fishing lodges + tournaments (Poco Bueno). Unregulated STRs. New public marina permit (~80 floating slips) + Calhoun County pipeline 31 projects/$95M. Town boxed by GIWW, bay, wetlands; Matagorda Island across the pass is a wildlife refuge = permanent undevelopable view/beach amenity — strongest "protected amenity" story on the mid-coast. No open-Gulf beachfront (boat-access only).
- **Bull:** Marina buildout + refuge-locked views + scarcity = classic quiet-appreciation setup. **Bear:** No Gulf beach, no airport, 6 hrs, narrow buyer pool (anglers).
- **Scores:** Access 5 | Value 6 | Momentum 5 | Catalysts 5 | Risk 5 → **26**

## 6. Rockport / Fulton (Aransas Co.)
- ~6.0–6.25 hr; CRP airport ~40 min (AA DFW ~4x daily). Rockport median sale $281K–$385K by source; $/sf +15.4%; Fulton median $585K (+36.3%, tiny waterfront-skewed sample). 7.4-mo supply, 31.8% of listings with price cuts. Rentals ADR $287–308, occ 30–42%, ~$24.8K/yr — 2nd-home/retiree market more than STR. Winter Texan season doubles population. STR registration (light). Harvey direct hit 2017 — rebuilt to modern code. Southern Living "best beach towns in the South" 2025. Art-town DNA (Rockport Center for the Arts) — most "30A-plausible" aesthetic on the coast, but demographically older. San Jose Island across the pass privately owned/undeveloped.
- **Bull:** Only town with art-town brand + flight access + post-Harvey new stock; Fulton waterfront ripping. **Bear:** No surf beach in town, mediocre STR yields, retiree demand ≠ boom demand.
- **Scores:** Access 6 | Value 6 | Momentum 7 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 5 → **30**

## 7. Port Aransas — the "already boomed?" benchmark (Nueces Co.)
- ~6.5–7 hr incl. ferry; CRP ~50 min + ferry. Zillow typical $599,646 (−1.9% YoY); Dec 2020 median list $409K → +45–50% over 5 yrs, ~2x over 10. Cinnamon Shore/Palmilla product $900K–$3M+. **26.4 months of inventory — massively oversupplied.** Rentals: ADR $316, occ ~53%, 2,463 listings — best occupancy north of SPI. Permits required, enforcement loose. Harvey rebuilt. Mustang Island hemmed by state park.
- **Verdict: yes, already boomed — now the comp, in a hangover.** Cautionary tale: STR-supply-driven booms mean-revert.
- **Scores:** Access 5 | Value 3 | Momentum 4 | Catalysts 4 | Risk 5 → **21**

## 8. North Padre Island / Corpus Christi (Nueces Co.)
- ~6.75–7 hr; CRP ~30 min (AA DFW 4–5x daily, WN DAL) — easiest fly-in beach on the list.
- **Prices:** Island median $438K Apr 2026 (+6.2% YoY) — one of the only coast markets still appreciating in 2026. Canal-front $500–800K; Gulf-view condos $250–500K.
- **Rentals:** occ 38–55%, ADR $189–205, revenue $22–37K; condos yield better given SFH ban.
- **STR regs — restrictive turn:** Corpus 2024 ordinance — $250/yr permit, 15% block-face cap, BAN on new non-owner STRs in single-family zones on the island (condos/multifamily exempt); 4 enforcement officers; most restrictive regime on the Texas coast for SFH.
- **THE CATALYST:** Whitecap NPI — $800M, 240-acre master-planned canal/resort community; 600 residences, first phase 199 homes from $900K, model homes Q4 2025, 7 bridges, 30-acre preserve — largest residential bet on the Texas coast. Plus Corpus energy growth (Exxon/SABIC, port expansion).
- **Bull:** Only still-appreciating market + $800M catalyst + real airport + Whitecap comp-pull. **Bear:** SFH STR ban guts the classic beach-house model; Corpus crime/industrial reputation drags the brand.
- **Scores:** Access 7 | Value 7 | Momentum 8 | Catalysts 8 | Risk 6 → **36**

## 9. Port Mansfield (Willacy Co.)
- ~8.5 hr — worst drive. HRL ~50 min; own GA airstrip. Avg ~$513,725; waterfront median list $512K; micro-market (~103 listings). ADR ~$300, occ ~36%. No STR regime. $21.4M USACE channel dredging completed Jul 2026 (17-ft restored). Leased-land grid (Navigation District ground leases — title diligence required); extreme supply constraint; world-class Lower Laguna Madre fishery.
- **Bull:** Dredging + SpaceX-era Valley wealth + absolute scarcity. **Bear:** 8.5 hrs kills the vacation thesis; leased land + no beach + no restaurants = no mainstream appreciation path.
- **Scores:** Access 2 | Value 6 | Momentum 4 | Catalysts 4 | Risk 6 → **22**

## 10. South Padre Island (Cameron Co.)
- ~9 hr drive — fly market: BRO (AA DFW 2–3x daily) ~30 min; HRL ~45 min.
- **Prices:** Median sale $535K Jun 2026; Zillow +4.5% YoY; condos $189K–$2.5M; Gulf-front condos from ~$300K = cheapest true beachfront ownership on the list. DOM 162 vs 79 — loosening fast despite price gains.
- **Rentals:** occ 47–53% (vs TX avg 33%), ADR $230–365, revenue $32–52K; 2,380 listings. Spring Break + Valley + Monterrey demand triangle.
- **STR regs:** Established and workable: $50 permit, 17% total lodging tax.
- **Momentum — strongest tourism tape in Texas:** HOT +10.4% YoY Jun 2025; room receipts $93M (1H25) → $115M (1H26), +24%. Catalysts: SpaceX/Starbase (city incorporated May 2025; ~$10B regional gross revenue since 2023; $99M/yr launch tourism; SpaceX bought 54 acres on SPI's north end 7/2026 + ~500 Cameron Co. parcels); second causeway ($700M–$1B, environmental clearance ~2026, construction possibly 2029).
- **Supply:** Developed strip ~5 mi of a 34-mi island; northern island county/undevelopable dunes — but SpaceX just showed big-money appetite there.
- **Bull:** SpaceX wealth + record tourism + causeway = only town with three independent boom catalysts; condo yields already work. **Bear:** 9 hrs (fly-only), party-brand stigma, condo insurance/assessment risk, DOM doubling says froth partly priced.
- **Scores:** Access 4 | Value 6 | Momentum 9 | Catalysts 9 | Risk 5 → **33**

## 11. Galveston West End (Jamaica Beach, Pirates Beach, Sea Isle, Pointe West)
- ~4.75–5.25 hr (I-45 straight shot — psychologically the easiest trip); HOU ~1 hr.
- **Prices:** Jamaica Beach median sale $497K (−22.7%, thin), $437/sf (+9.3% — highest $/sf on the list); Pirates Beach beachfront $800K–$2M+. Island: 8M visitors/yr, $1.2B spend.
- **Rentals:** ADR ~$250–300, occ ~40%; big houses $400–800/nt peak; 15% HOT.
- **STR regs — tightening:** Nov 2025 ordinance — $250 annual registration, 3-strike license revocation, owner liability. Jamaica Beach separate city, own 2023 ordinance. HOAs can overrule.
- **Risk:** Outside the seawall — chronic erosion, GLO rolling easement issues; Ike surge history; wind stacks $4.5–7K on older homes.
- **Momentum:** $700–800M Margaritaville resort (95 acres, 15-story hotel + 278 cottages, tower from Q2 2026, complete ~2030) — but EAST end; west end gets halo only.
- **Bull:** Shortest drive + deepest rental demand pool + Margaritaville halo + highest $/sf. **Bear:** Regulatory ratchet + HOA vetoes + outside-seawall erosion/insurance; already fully discovered by Houston — no discovery discount.
- **Scores:** Access 9 | Value 5 | Momentum 6 | Catalysts 6 | Risk 3 → **29**

**Key observations:** (1) Whole coast mid-correction — 2026 is a buyer's-leverage entry window except still-appreciating North Padre and tourism-ripping SPI. (2) The two highest scorers are both fly-markets; the best drive markets carry the worst insurance/erosion drag. (3) "Next Port Aransas" by catalyst weight: North Padre (Whitecap $800M) and SPI (SpaceX + causeway); "next Rockport-style quiet appreciation": Port O'Connor. (4) Port A's 26-month glut is the cautionary tale — supply-capped towns don't build their way into gluts. (5) No Texas town has 30A's walkable-upscale DNA except Cinnamon Shore (Port A) and under-construction Whitecap.
