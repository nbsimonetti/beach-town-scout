## Method in one paragraph

Five parallel research agents (Aug 2026) first reverse-engineered what the blockbuster beach towns looked like 5–10 years *before* their booms — the repeatable fingerprints are a patient master developer assembling land, a committed access project (bridge/road/airport), a wealthy feeder metro within reach, and a hard supply cap. Those fingerprints became a 14-indicator checklist, which was then applied to 55+ candidate towns across the US Gulf, Atlantic, Pacific, Mexico, and the Caribbean using current (2024–2026) price, rental, regulatory, insurance, and catalyst data. Each town gets five 1–10 sub-scores; a Dallas-weighted composite ranks the field, and a separate upside index maps to calibrated probability bands for ≥7%/yr nominal appreciation over the next decade.

## Headline results

- **Best drive-market value:** Bay St. Louis, MS
- **The structural "next 30A":** Port St. Joe / Cape San Blas, FL (St. Joe Co. — 30A's creator — owns 165,000 acres there)
- **Best Texas catalyst play:** North Padre Island ($800M Whitecap development; only Gulf market still appreciating in 2026)
- **Highest international torque:** Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca (+111–160% in 5 yrs; highway + airport catalysts still landing; title risk is existential — clean-title parcels only)
- **Best international access + title:** Ambergris Caye, Belize (~6 hrs door-to-door, fee-simple English-law title)
- **Cautionary benchmark:** Tulum (−48% condo correction — oversupply beats any airport)

*Research date: August 2026. Point-in-time data; several dossier figures are flagged as unverified estimates. Not investment advice.*
